2. The short version

  • Mythic Bricks has no accounts of its own and no servers of its own. We do not receive or store your personal data on any system of ours.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone and, if you use Google Play Games, in your Google Play Games cloud save.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → "Ad privacy choices".
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details.
  • We do not sell your data.

3. What is saved on your phone

Your game progress and settings (levels, upgrades, gems, Rift results, event progress, sound and language settings) are stored locally on your device. So that a purchase is never delivered twice or lost, the game also keeps the order numbers of your Google Play purchases with your save. This data stays on your phone unless you use the Google Play Games cloud save (section 4). Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

4. Google Play Games

If you have a Google Play Games profile, the game signs you in automatically through Google Play Games Services, provided by Google Ireland Limited. This is used for:

  • Your gamer profile: your Play Games player ID, gamer name and avatar, so the game can greet you and link your progress.
  • Achievements: which achievements you have unlocked.
  • Leaderboards: your best floor in the weekly Rift. Who can see your profile and scores is controlled by your Play Games visibility settings.
  • Cloud save: a copy of your game progress (including the purchase order numbers from section 3), so you can continue on a new phone.

This data is stored by Google in your Google account. Google also collects technical analytics and diagnostics about the Play Games service. You can play without a Play Games profile. You can delete the data of single games, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Google Play Games app or in your Google account. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads (which you choose to watch) and occasional full-screen ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes:

  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be estimated,
  • interactions with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps, video views),
  • diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK.

Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are used. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use data for limited purposes such as frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → "Ad privacy choices". You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

If you buy the Ad-free pass, the game no longer shows full-screen ads and rewarded bonuses are given without an ad. See how Google uses data in advertising.

6. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Gem packs, the Gem Vault, the bundles, the Starter pack, the Midas charm, the Ascension fund, the Event pass, the Ad-free pass and the 30-day passes (Mythic Pass and Supply Pass) are bought through Google Play's billing system. Everything is a one-time purchase, nothing is a subscription: nothing renews by itself. The game keeps, with your save, until when a pass runs and which daily rewards were claimed. Google processes your payment details; we never receive them. The game only receives what it needs to deliver the purchase: the product, the order number and whether the payment went through. This purchase history is kept with your save (sections 3 and 4) so that restored purchases are recognised. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

7. Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules reminders on your phone (for example when your offline earnings are full or when the weekly Rift resets). They are created on the device itself; no data is sent anywhere for this. You can turn them off in the game or in Android settings.

8. Sharing

When you tap "Share", the game draws a picture of your result on your phone and hands it, with a short text and the store link, to your phone's share menu. Where it goes is up to you. We receive nothing.

9. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Providing the game, cloud saves, achievements, leaderboards and purchases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract with you).
  • Personalised advertising: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).
  • Non-personalised advertising, fraud prevention and diagnostics: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in financing a free game and keeping it secure).

10. Transfers outside the EU

Google may process data on servers outside the European Union, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for such transfers.

11. How long data is kept

Data on your phone is kept until you uninstall the game, clear its storage or reset your progress. Data held by Google (Play Games, AdMob, Google Play) is kept according to Google's own retention policies and can be deleted as described above.

12. Children

Mythic Bricks is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process data of children under 13.

13. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Since we keep no data on systems of our own, most requests are answered fastest through the settings described above or by Google directly. You are always welcome to write to us at topirceanilie90@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live or the authority responsible for us: Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg, Germany.

14. Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects your data, this policy will be updated and the date at the top changed.
